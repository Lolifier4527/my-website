How it works

Ready to play in under a minute

1
Choose the cast
Type a classmate's name for the Hero, the Best Friend, Grandma, the Hotel Manager and the Chef, and pick a skin tone for each. Names stay on that device only.
2
Read the story
Tap Next to move through nine short chapters. Press 🔊 Read or turn on Auto-read to have every line read aloud.
3
Make the choices
Learners decide what happens next. Some choices check what they've read, and a wrong answer gives a friendly hint to try again.
For teachers

Built for the classroom

🧒
Learners are the stars
Every character can be renamed, so the whole class can see themselves in the story.
🔊
Read-aloud support
A friendly narrator voice reads each line, which helps emerging readers follow along.
🧠
Reading checks
Comprehension questions are built into the story, with gentle hints instead of game-overs.
📱
Any device
Runs in the browser on phones, tablets, laptops and interactive whiteboards. Nothing to install.
